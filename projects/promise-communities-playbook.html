Promise Communities Playbook

Utah's Promise · 2026

Overview

Wrote and designed the Promise Communities Playbook, a practical guide for communities building cradle-to-career partnerships that help young people and families move toward economic mobility. The playbook covers 12 topics, from the first conversations about whether a community is ready to begin to the work of sustaining results over time, and gives local leaders and the coaches who support them a shared roadmap.

Context

Utah's Promise supports a growing number of emerging Promise Communities across the state, from urban neighborhoods to rural towns. Each community starts in a different place, and the support they received often depended on which staff member they worked with. Useful tools and training materials existed, but they were spread across the organization. The playbook brings that knowledge together so every community can get consistent, well-timed support at whatever stage it is in.

Approach

The playbook is built around a developmental pathway: six phases that describe what progress looks like as a partnership grows, with concrete milestones at each phase. The pathway works as a diagnostic tool, helping communities and coaches identify where a partnership is today so the right support can follow.

Key activities included:

  • Designed the six-phase developmental pathway, with observable milestones for each phase and a clear picture of the impact on young people and families at every stage
  • Wrote 12 chapters across five focus areas: purpose and approaches; backbone and governance; place and partnership; data and strategies; and sustainability and funding
  • Covered topics including defining a community's geography, building partnerships, using data to make decisions, choosing strategies, planning and funding the work, and telling the partnership's story
  • Turned six widely used methods for leadership, facilitation, and improvement into short, practical two-page guides
  • Reviewed existing organizational materials and national models to bring the best of each into one coherent resource
  • Led visual design and layout, including diagrams, a phases-at-a-glance one-pager, and a consistent style across the full document

Outcomes

  • A complete 12-chapter playbook, plus phase guides listing the tools to use at each milestone
  • A developmental pathway that helps diagnose where each community is so coaches can tailor their support
  • Approximately 70 internal tools, plus external resources, linked directly in the playbook, giving communities and staff one place to find what they need
  • A guide written to work for place-based partnerships anywhere, with Utah-specific guidance set apart
